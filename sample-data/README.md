# Brackenholt Energy (fictional)

Brackenholt Energy is a 900-person gas and electricity supplier for homes and small businesses in
the North of England. Head office and the customer contact centre are in Sheffield; smart-meter
engineers work from home and a van across Yorkshire, the North East and the North West; a small
software team builds the customer app and the billing tools, mostly remotely.

Every skill in this pack uses Brackenholt for its sample input, so the pack reads as one place.
The vacancies below are very different on purpose: the skills work the same way for volume
hiring, a technical role, a field role, a first management job and a senior hire.

## Structure

| Area | People | Who's in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer operations, Sheffield | about 420 | customer advisers, team leaders, contact centre managers, a Head of Customer Operations |
| Field operations | about 260 | smart-meter engineers, field supervisors, planners |
| Technology | about 70 | software developers, testers, a data team, IT support |
| Head office | about 150 | finance, regulation and compliance, trading, people team |

The people team includes a Head of People, a talent acquisition lead, two talent acquisition
partners, a recruitment coordinator and HR advisers. Hiring managers do most of the interviewing.
Hiring runs through an applicant tracking system (ATS); first interviews are usually by video,
final interviews in person or by video.
