# Customer Adviser assessment: reply to an adjustment request (recruitment coordinator's draft)

## The assessment

- Customer Adviser, Sheffield contact centre, rolling intake. Candidate C is booked on the assessment session on Wednesday 18 November 2026, 13:00 to 15:30, at Brackenholt House, Sheffield.
- Three parts:
  1. Group exercise, 30 minutes. Six candidates discuss how to sort out a customer's billing complaint. Two team leaders observe and score "works with others" and "listens and responds to customers".
  2. Call role-play, 20 minutes. A team leader plays a customer ringing about a high bill.
  3. Structured interview, 30 minutes, with the contact centre manager. Five questions, spoken aloud.
- The panel for the session: the contact centre manager and two team leaders.
- The recruitment coordinator handles adjustment requests.
