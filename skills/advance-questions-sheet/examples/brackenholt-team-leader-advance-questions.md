## Draft sheet

**Your Team Leader interview: the questions we'll ask**

We're sharing the main interview questions so you can think about your examples beforehand. The interview lasts 60 minutes with the contact centre manager and a talent acquisition partner, and we'll ask follow-up questions on the day. You're welcome to bring notes.

**1. Leading a team.** Tell us about a time you led a team, or a group of colleagues, through a busy or difficult day. What did you do, and what happened?
What the panel will score highly: setting clear priorities at the start of the shift, moving people between queues as demand changes, checking in with quieter team members, and a clear result such as service levels recovered by the end of the day.

**2. Coaching.** Tell us about a time you used BRITE scores and a side-by-side to coach an adviser whose quality had dropped.
What the panel will score highly: using the BRITE breakdown to find the one behaviour to work on, agreeing a goal with the adviser, a follow-up side-by-side within a fortnight, and scores that improved.

**3. Attendance.** Describe a time you had to raise a concern with someone about their attendance or timekeeping. How did you handle it fairly?
What the panel will score highly: talking privately and promptly, asking about the reasons before deciding anything, following the attendance policy, and treating the person the same as anyone else in the team.

**4. Complaints.** Tell us about a complaint you dealt with where the customer was very unhappy. What did you do?
What the panel will score highly: letting the customer explain in full, owning the problem, a clear next step with a date, and following up to check it was fixed.

**Preparing:** answers work best as a real example: the situation, what you did and what happened.
