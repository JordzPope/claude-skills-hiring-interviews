# Software Developer hiring: candidate AI use statement (engineering manager's draft for the careers page)

## How the Software Developer process works

- Two remote Software Developer roles in the software team, which builds the customer app and the billing tools.
- Stages: an application through the ATS; a take-home task; a 45-minute live coding session; a final interview by video with the engineering manager and a talent acquisition partner.
- Take-home task: fix a bug and add a small feature in a sample billing app, about two hours. The task instructions say candidates may use any tools they'd use at work, including AI coding assistants, and should be ready to explain their choices.
- Live coding: the candidate pairs with a developer on Microsoft Teams, in a shared online code editor, on a small problem, and talks through their thinking. The developer also asks follow-up questions about the take-home.
- The ATS has an AI assistant that writes a short summary of each CV and suggests a match score against the job description. The engineering manager sees both when deciding who to invite to the take-home.
- All interviews run on Teams with cameras on. They aren't recorded or transcribed, and no other software runs during them.
- Adjustment requests go to the recruitment coordinator.

## Draft statement

**Our position on AI**

At Brackenholt we value authenticity. AI tools of any kind must not be used at any stage of our recruitment process, from writing your CV to your final interview. We want to see the real you.

Our interview platform uses AI detection technology that monitors eye movement, background audio and pasted code, and flags candidates who may be using AI.

Any candidate found to have used AI will be removed from the process immediately and may be barred from applying for future roles at Brackenholt. We will know.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact the talent acquisition team.
