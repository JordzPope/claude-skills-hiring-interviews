# Smart-Meter Engineer recruitment: candidate survey (draft v1)

Drafted by the recruitment coordinator for the talent acquisition partner to approve, Wednesday 28 October 2026.

## About the round

Brackenholt is hiring 12 smart-meter engineers to cover Yorkshire, the North East and the North West. The process:

1. Phone screen with the recruitment coordinator.
2. Video interview with a field supervisor, 45 minutes.
3. Practical assessment at the Wakefield training centre, half a day: safe isolation, fitting a meter to a training board, and a role-play with a "customer" at their front door. Sessions ran on 6, 13 and 20 October 2026, eight candidates per session, each candidate assessed by one of the four field supervisors.

So far 61 people have had a video interview, 24 have attended a practical assessment and 12 have accepted offers.

## Send plan

The field supervisor hands out the survey on day one of induction, Monday 9 November, to the 12 new engineers, who fill it in on the induction-room tablet before the first session starts.

## Intro text

Welcome to Brackenholt! This anonymous survey helps us make our recruitment the best in the industry. It takes about five minutes.

## Questions

1. Which practical assessment session did you attend? (6 October / 13 October / 20 October)
2. Which field supervisor assessed you? (drop-down of the four field supervisors' names)
3. Did your interview invitation tell you everything you needed to know? (Yes / Partly / No)
4. Did you need any adjustments for the practical assessment? (Yes / No) If yes, please tell us the condition or disability the adjustment was for, so we can plan better next time.
5. What did you enjoy most about our friendly, well-organised assessment day?
6. How long did you wait to hear the outcome after your practical assessment? (Less than a week / One to two weeks / More than two weeks)
7. Having seen how we work, how likely are you to choose Brackenholt as your own energy supplier? (0 to 10)
8. Anything else you'd like to tell us?
