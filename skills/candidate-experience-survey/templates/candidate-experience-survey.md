# Candidate experience survey: [vacancy or process]

## Send plan

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who receives it | every candidate interviewed at any stage, successful or not, internal or external: [stages] |
| When | [two to five] working days after their outcome message |
| Reminder | [five] working days after sending |
| Closes | [ten] working days after sending |
| Sent by | [TA team mailbox], the same message and link for everyone, outside any induction or meeting |
| Tool and settings | [tool]; name and email collection off; response receipts off; one link, never tied to the ATS |
| Smallest group reported | [10] responses |
| Owner | [role] |

## Intro (shown before question 1)

Thank you for interviewing with us for [role]. We'd like to know what it was like, so we can fix what didn't work. The decision on your application has already been made, and nothing you tell us here can be linked to you or change it: the survey is anonymous and doesn't record names or email addresses. It takes about [three] minutes. If you'd like it in another format, or help to fill it in, contact [mailbox]. We'll share what we've changed by [date] at [where].

## Questions

**The invitation**

1. Did your invitation tell you the date, the format, who you'd meet, how long it would take and what to prepare?
   - Yes, all of it
   - Some of it
   - No

**Adjustments**

2. Did you know you could ask for adjustments to the interview?
   - Yes
   - No
3. If you asked for adjustments, were they in place?
   - I didn't need any
   - Yes, fully
   - Partly
   - No

**Questions in advance** (include only if you share them)

4. Did the questions or themes arrive in time to help you prepare?
   - Yes
   - They arrived too late to help
   - I didn't receive them

**The interviewers**

5. Did the interviewers explain how the interview would run?
   - Yes
   - Partly
   - No
6. Did you get a fair chance to show what you can do?
   - Yes
   - Partly
   - No
7. Were you asked anything that didn't seem relevant to the job?
   - No
   - Yes (you can tell us more in the last question)

**The wait**

8. Which of these matches the wait for your outcome?
   - I was told when I'd hear, and heard by then
   - I was told when I'd hear, and heard later
   - I wasn't told when to expect it

**Feedback** (include only if you offer it)

9. If you asked for feedback, was it specific enough to use?
   - I didn't ask for feedback
   - Yes
   - Partly
   - No

**What to fix**

10. What one thing should we change? [open box]

Optional, only where each option will have [10] or more responses: "How far did you get? First interview / Final interview."

**Thank you.** [One line: thanks, and where they'll see what changes.]

## Analysis plan

| Step | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Base | every question reported as n of N responses, with the response rate |
| Threshold | a vacancy, stage or outcome group reported only with [10] or more responses |
| Open answers | names and identifying details removed, then themed, with each theme counted |
| The interviewers | read across all panels; fixes go into the Interviewer Briefing and practice for everyone |
| Actions | [two to four] fixes, each with an owner by role and a date |
| Share back | what you changed, by [date], where candidates will see it |
