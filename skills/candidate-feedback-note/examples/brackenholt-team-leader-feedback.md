# Team Leader, Customer Operations: feedback for Candidate C

## Part 1: Panel's decision record (exported from the ATS)

Vacancy: Team Leader, Customer Operations, Sheffield contact centre (4 roles)
Stage: final interview, Thursday 22 October 2026, in person
Panel: contact centre manager (chair), a team leader from the complaints team, talent acquisition partner (notes)
Candidate C: internal; customer adviser in the billing team for three years

Scoring: 1 to 5 on each criterion against the scoring guide agreed at intake (1 no evidence, 3 sound, 5 strong). A score of 3 or more is needed on every criterion.

| Criterion | Agreed score | Evidence notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coaching advisers to improve | 4 | Described coaching a new adviser on call wrap-up: listened in side by side twice a week, agreed one change at a time, checked the wrap-time report each Friday. Wrap time fell from about 90 seconds to 45 over six weeks. |
| Managing performance and attendance fairly | 2 | Example: an adviser was late most Mondays for a month. C covered the start of their calls and had one informal word. Asked what they'd do if it carried on: "I'd pass it to my manager." No mention of recording it, the attendance policy or a follow-up conversation. Probe on treating two advisers the same way: general answer, no example. |
| Planning the shift to meet service levels | 3 | Moved breaks using the real-time adherence screen when the queue went over 20 calls. Didn't mention the forecast or the skill mix on the shift. |
| Handling escalated complaints | 4 | Took over a call from a customer whose prepayment meter had run out of credit, arranged emergency credit, recorded the customer as vulnerable with their agreement, and called back the next day to check. |

Decision: not appointed. Reason: managing performance and attendance is essential for the role, and C's evidence scored 2, below the 3 needed. The panel agreed C gave strong evidence on coaching and on escalated complaints.

Feedback: C asked by email on Monday 26 October for written feedback. The chair will draft it and the talent acquisition partner will check it before it goes.

Future vacancies: not discussed by the panel.
