# Person specification: Finance Business Partner (draft from the Head of Finance)

| --- | --- |

| Closing date | 21 November 2026 |

## About the role

The Finance Business Partner works with the Head of Customer Operations and the field operations leaders, who between them hold most of Brackenholt's operating budget. They build the monthly forecast with budget holders, explain variances, and model the cost of decisions such as adding a contact centre shift or changing engineer rotas. They write a monthly finance paper for the operations leadership meeting and present it in person. Meetings with budget holders are in the Sheffield office or on video.

## Essential

1. CIMA, ACCA or ACA qualified
2. A 2:1 degree or above in accounting, finance or economics
3. At least 10 years' post-qualification experience
4. Experience of business partnering with operational budget holders, including building forecasts and challenging spend
5. Advanced Excel, including building financial models from scratch
6. Native English speaker, for writing board papers
7. Full UK driving licence

## Desirable

- Experience in energy, utilities or another regulated sector
- Experience with Power BI or a similar reporting tool
