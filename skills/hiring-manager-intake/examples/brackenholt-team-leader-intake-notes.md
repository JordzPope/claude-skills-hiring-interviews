## Why we're hiring

Two team leaders have moved to the new complaints team, and the floor grows by two teams in January to take the smart-meter rollout calls. Each team leader runs a team of 12 to 14 customer advisers on the billing and payments lines, on the Monday to Saturday rota.

The contact centre manager sees this as a first step into management: "I want our best advisers to go for it. That's how I started." Internal and external candidates can apply.

## What success looks like after a year

In the manager's words: "We need rockstars. Natural leaders who are a good culture fit for the floor and hit the numbers."

- Team hitting its targets
- Advisers happy
- Fewer escalations coming to me

## What the job involves

- Daily huddles and live floor support, including taking escalated calls
- Monthly one-to-ones and call listening with each adviser
- Return-to-work meetings and the first stages of the absence and performance processes, with HR adviser support
- Weekly rota and leave planning with the planning team
- A weekly report on team results to the contact centre manager

## Must-haves

1. At least two years' experience as a people manager
2. Has handled escalated customer complaints and can show how they calmed the customer and resolved the issue
3. Gives clear feedback to colleagues on their work, for example as a senior adviser, buddy or trainer
4. Internal applicants: no more than three sick days in the last 12 months. For external candidates, we'll ask about their sickness record at the first interview.

## Nice-to-haves

- Experience in energy or utilities
- A coaching qualification

## Process

- Applications close 14 November.
- Stage 1, w/c 17 November: 30-minute video interview with the TA partner and a team leader.
- Stage 2, w/c 24 November: in-person interview plus a 20-minute coaching exercise (giving feedback to an adviser, played by a team leader), with the contact centre manager and an HR adviser.
- Adjustments: every invitation tells candidates to contact the recruitment coordinator.
- Candidates hear back within three working days of each stage.
- Start date 5 January 2027, after a two-week induction for external hires.

The manager said one of the four roles is already spoken for: the senior adviser who has been acting up as team leader on Team 6 since June. "I've told them it's theirs. They don't need to do the coaching exercise, I've watched them do it for months. Their stage 2 can be a quick chat."

## Pay

£28,500 to £30,500, plus the quarterly team bonus. Confirmed with the people team.
