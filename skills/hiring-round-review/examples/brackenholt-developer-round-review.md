# Software Developer round review (draft)

Prepared by a talent acquisition partner for the engineering manager and the talent acquisition lead, Friday 30 October 2026.

Round: Software Developer, 2 roles, remote. Advert opened Tuesday 1 September 2026; both offers accepted by Friday 23 October 2026.

## Summary

We filled both roles within 31 working days of application. Every candidate got an answer this round. The live coding interview cut the field from 12 to 5, and the numbers show it disadvantages women, so we propose replacing it.

## 3. Scoring consistency

Live coding: two interviewers per candidate, the engineering manager plus one of the two senior developers. Each scored 1 to 5 on problem solving, code quality and explaining decisions, before the panel agreed a score.

| Interviewer | Candidates scored | Median: problem solving | Median: code quality | Median: explaining decisions | Times 2+ points from the agreed score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ravi (engineering manager) | 12 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 |
| Gemma (senior developer) | 7 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 0 |
| Owen (senior developer) | 5 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 1 |

Ravi's scores ran about a point above the agreed score on all three criteria. Owen scored code quality lower than the others, though with five candidates this may be chance. We suggest a calibration session for all three before the next round, using two sample answers written for the exercise.
