# Software Developer (2 roles, remote): interview integrity plan (draft)

Prepared by: engineering manager, with the talent acquisition partner, for the talent acquisition lead to approve
Date: Tuesday 27 October 2026
Applies from: the next developer round, opening Monday 16 November 2026

## Why we need this

In the last developer round, two candidates gave polished answers on the first video interview but couldn't talk through their own take-home code at the final stage. We want one approach the whole panel follows before the next round opens.

## Stages

1. **Take-home task:** a small feature for one of the billing tools, about three hours, submitted through the ATS.
2. **First interview by video (45 minutes):** engineering manager and a senior developer. Structured questions on the four criteria, from the interview guide.
3. **Final interview by video (60 minutes):** a walk-through of the candidate's own take-home code, a 20-minute live extension of that code with their screen shared, and two structured questions.

## Checks

| Stage | Check | Who | Candidates told? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First interview | Candidate holds photo ID to the camera at the start; coordinator notes "seen and matched" and keeps no copy | Recruitment coordinator joins for the first two minutes | Yes, in the invitation |
| First and final interviews | Camera on for the whole interview, with no exceptions | Interviewers | Yes, in the invitation |
| First and final interviews | The video platform's "attention monitor" add-on, which flags any candidate whose gaze leaves the screen more than 8 times in 10 minutes; flagged interviews go to the engineering manager to review | Recruitment coordinator switches it on | Yes, in the invitation |
| Any stage, for candidates whose CV shows they studied or worked outside the UK | A second ID check and a 360-degree room scan before the interview starts | Recruitment coordinator | No |
| Final interview | Walk-through of the candidate's own take-home code: "Why did you structure it this way? What would you change with another day?" | Senior developer | Yes, in the invitation |
| Final interview | Live extension of the candidate's own take-home code, screen shared | Senior developer | Yes, in the invitation |
| After a conditional offer | Right to work check; coordinator confirms the person matches the ID seen at the first interview | Recruitment coordinator | Yes, in the offer letter |

## Planned probes

For every structured question, interviewers use the probes written in the interview guide: "What did you do next?", "What was the hardest part?" and "What would you do differently now?" Every candidate gets the same probes.

## Adjustments

Candidates can ask the recruitment coordinator for adjustments before any stage, for example extra time on the take-home task, the questions in advance, or a screen reader during the live extension.

## If an interviewer has a concern

If an interviewer believes a candidate is using AI or isn't the real applicant, they should stop the interview, tell the candidate they have been caught breaking our rules on AI, and end the call. The recruitment coordinator then marks the application as "Fraud" in the ATS and sends the standard rejection email.

## Wording for the invitation

"At the start of your first interview we'll ask you to hold your photo ID up to the camera. Cameras stay on throughout, and our video platform monitors attention during the call. In the final interview you'll talk us through your take-home task and extend it live with your screen shared. If you need any adjustments, tell the recruitment coordinator and we'll talk them through with you."
