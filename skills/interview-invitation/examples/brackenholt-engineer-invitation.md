# Smart-Meter Engineer, second stage: invitation (recruitment coordinator's draft)

## From the interview plan

- Second stage for the 12 Smart-Meter Engineer roles: a practical assessment, then a structured interview. 18 candidates, booked across Monday 9 to Wednesday 11 November 2026, two sessions a day.
- Each session runs 08:30 to 11:45. The practical takes 90 minutes: a single-phase meter exchange on a training board, including a safe isolation check. Then a 15-minute break and a 45-minute interview.
- Panel: a field supervisor and a talent acquisition partner. The interview has five questions on working safely, looking after customers in their own homes, and planning a day's jobs.
- Venue: the engineer training workshop, Brackenholt House, Calder Row, Sheffield. Report to the workshop entrance on the visitor car park side of the building; main reception is on the other side. The workshop is on the ground floor, step-free from the car park, with an accessible toilet next door. Free parking for candidates.
- PPE is provided on the day. Candidates need to wear sturdy, closed-toe shoes.
- Right to work checks happen after a conditional offer.
- Adjustment requests go to the recruitment coordinator.
- Invitations go out from the ATS on Thursday 5 November 2026 at 15:00.
