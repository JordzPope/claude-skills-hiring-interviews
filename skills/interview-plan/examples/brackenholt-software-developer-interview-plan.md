# Interview plan: Software Developer (2 roles, remote), draft from the engineering manager

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Hiring manager | Engineering manager |
| Team | Customer app and billing tools, Technology |
| Closing date | 20 November 2026 |
| Target start | February 2027 |

## Criteria (from the agreed success profile)

A. Writes clear, working, tested code for a feature in the billing tools
B. Reviews colleagues' code and gives specific, usable comments
C. Finds and fixes a fault in live code methodically
D. Explains technical choices and trade-offs to non-technical colleagues, such as the contact centre product owner
E. Plans and shares their own work in a remote team: progress updates, early warnings, asking for help

## Stages

| Stage | Format | Who | Length | Dates | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Screening call | Video | TA partner | 20 min | w/c 23 November | Notice period, salary expectations, working pattern, the candidate's questions about the role. Not scored. |
| 2. Take-home task | Build a small feature against a sample billing API, with tests | Marked by a senior developer | 3 hours suggested, 5 days to return | sent w/c 30 November | A |
| 3. Technical interview | Video. Engineering manager leads, senior developer takes notes. | Engineering manager and a senior developer | 75 min | w/c 7 December | Extend the take-home live with a new requirement, to hear the candidate's own reasoning (A, C); debug a failing test (C); "What makes a good code review?" (B); "Tell us about a time your work was running late in a remote team. What did you do?" (E) |
| 4. Final | Video | Engineering manager | 30 min | w/c 14 December | Informal culture fit chat. No set questions; a feel for whether they'll fit in with the team. |

## Notes

- Candidates with a strong GitHub profile, or referred by one of our developers, skip the take-home and go straight to the technical interview.
- The take-home brief says what help is allowed, including AI tools, in line with our candidate AI statement.
- Panel members score on their own in the ATS before the debrief.
- Candidates hear the outcome within five working days of each stage.
- Adjustments: every invitation tells candidates to contact the recruitment coordinator.
- Right to work checks for the two people offered jobs, before they start.
- Notes and scores are kept in the ATS for six months after the decision.
