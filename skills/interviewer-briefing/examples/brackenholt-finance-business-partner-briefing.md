# Panel briefing: Finance Business Partner, final interviews

To: Head of Finance, Director of Regulation, talent acquisition partner
From: talent acquisition partner
Interviews: Wednesday 18 November, head office, Sheffield, Meeting Room 4. Candidate C joins by Teams; the talent acquisition partner sends the link and will phone the candidate if the connection drops.

## Who does what

- **Chair: Head of Finance.** Welcomes each candidate, introduces the panel, explains the format, keeps time, asks questions 1 and 2, and invites the candidate's questions at the end.
- **Director of Regulation.** Asks questions 3 and 4.
- **Talent acquisition partner.** Takes the main notes and asks question 5.

Everyone writes their own notes as well, on the note sheet.

Each interview: welcome 5 minutes, the candidate's case presentation 15 minutes, questions 30 minutes, the candidate's questions 10 minutes.

## What you'll use

The interview guide (five questions, in the same order for every candidate) and the scoring guide (1 to 5 for each criterion) are attached. Note sheets are in the panel folder. Use the probes in the guide and keep to the questions as written.

## Questions to avoid

Don't ask about age, marriage or children, religion, or where someone is "originally from". The Director of Regulation usually asks how many days' sickness the candidate had in their last job, as a reliability check; that's fine to keep.

## Adjustments

Candidate B has dyslexia and anxiety and takes medication for it. They've asked for the case study 24 hours in advance and for each question to be shown on screen as well as read out. Please be patient if they seem nervous.

## Scoring and the debrief

After each candidate, the Director of Regulation will give their view first, then we'll go round the panel and agree a score for each criterion together.

## Notes and confidentiality

Please post your notes and scores in the Finance Leadership Team channel in Teams after each interview, so everyone can see how the day is going. Keep candidates' details confidential outside Brackenholt.
