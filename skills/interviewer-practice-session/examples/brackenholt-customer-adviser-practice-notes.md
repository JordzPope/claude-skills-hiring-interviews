# Notes: interviewer practice before the Customer Adviser intake

From: talent acquisition partner, after a call with the Head of Customer Operations

- 30 Customer Adviser roles on a rolling intake from January. About 10 contact centre managers and team leaders will interview, most of them for the first time, about half of the interviews by video.
- We've got the interview guide and the scoring guide (1 to 5, anchors at 1, 3 and 5) for the four criteria: finding out what the customer needs; explaining something clearly; staying calm with an upset customer; getting things right first time.
- Slot: Thursday 3 December, 2pm, one hour, Training Room 2. Two or three people dialling in on Teams.
- Plan:
  - Welcome and why we're doing this: 10 mins
  - Role play asking the questions in threes: 15 mins
  - Watch the Teams recording of last month's interview with the candidate who withdrew (we kept it, and there's a good mix of strong and weak answers in it), so it feels real: 10 mins
  - Then we all call out our scores and agree the right one together: 10 mins
  - Head of Customer Ops wants 10 mins on "reading people": the first two minutes tell you most of what you need, and she wants managers to trust their gut on that
  - Quick quiz on questions you can't ask: 10 mins
  - Q&A: 10 mins
- Send the interview guide and scoring guide round the day before.
