# Customer Adviser hiring: candidate messages in the ATS (current set-up)

Exported by the recruitment coordinator for the talent acquisition partner to review, Tuesday 27 October 2026.

## The vacancy

Customer Adviser, Sheffield contact centre. 30 roles, recruited on a rolling basis. Full-time or part-time, 16 to 37.5 hours a week, on shifts between 8am and 8pm, Monday to Saturday. About 240 applications a month.

Essential criteria: clear, friendly communication with customers; accuracy with figures and account details; staying calm when a customer is upset; willingness to learn new systems.

## The process

1. Apply online: a CV and four screening questions.
2. Screening against the essential criteria by the recruitment coordinator.
3. First interview by video, 30 minutes, with a team leader.
4. Assessment session at the Sheffield contact centre, half a day: a group exercise and a call role-play, with the contact centre manager and two team leaders.
5. Offer by phone from the contact centre manager.

## Messages set up in the ATS

| Stage | Message | When it goes | Sent from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| After screening | Template 1 | Batched on the last working day of each month | noreply@brackenholt-careers.co.uk |
| After first interview | Template 2 | Within 3 working days of the interview | noreply@brackenholt-careers.co.uk |
| After assessment session | No template set up | Offers are made by phone | |

The assessment session invitation ends: "If you haven't heard from us within three weeks of your session, please assume you have been unsuccessful on this occasion."

## Template 1: after screening

Subject: Your application: Customer Adviser

Dear Applicant,

Thank you for your interest in the Customer Adviser position at Brackenholt Energy.

We regret to inform you that your application has been unsuccessful on this occasion. Due to the high volume of applications we receive, we are unable to provide individual feedback.

We will keep your CV on file for 12 months and will contact you if a suitable vacancy arises.

We wish you every success in your job search.

Brackenholt Energy Recruitment Team

Please do not reply to this email, as this mailbox is not monitored.
