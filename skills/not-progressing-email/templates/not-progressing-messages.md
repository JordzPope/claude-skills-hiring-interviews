# Not progressing messages: [vacancy or process]

## Stage timeline

| Stage where a candidate stops | Trigger | Send by | Sender (role) | Channel | Feedback offered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| After application | screening decision recorded in the ATS | [5] working days after the decision | [recruitment coordinator] | ATS email from [named, monitored mailbox] | [none / a few lines on request] |
| After [test or task] | result recorded | [5] working days after the result | [role] | [channel] | [policy] |
| After first interview | panel decision recorded | [3] working days after the interview | [talent acquisition partner] | email | [short feedback on request] |
| After final interview | panel decision recorded | [2] working days after the interview, call first | [hiring manager] calls; [talent acquisition partner] emails the same day | phone, then email | [written feedback offered] |
| Vacancy filled, paused or withdrawn | decision to close or pause | [2] working days after the decision | [talent acquisition partner] | email | n/a |
| Holding update | a decision will miss the date the candidate was given | by that date | the sender for that stage | email | n/a |

## 1. After application

**Subject:** Your application for [role] at [organisation]

Hi [first name],

Thank you for applying for the [role] role. I'm sorry to tell you that we won't be taking your application to interview.

[Only if true for everyone at this stage: We had [number] applications and invited the people whose answers showed [criterion] most clearly.]

[Feedback line, per policy: We can't give individual feedback at this stage. / If you'd like a few lines of feedback, reply by [date] and I'll send them.]

[Future-roles consent wording, if used: see section 6.]

Best wishes,
[name], [role], [organisation]
[reply address]

## 2. After first interview

**Subject:** Your interview for [role]

Hi [first name],

Thank you for your time on [day and date], and for talking us through [something specific to the stage, such as the call role-play]. I'm sorry to tell you that we've decided not to take you forward to the next stage.

[Feedback offer: If you'd like feedback, reply to this email by [date] and [role] will send it within [five] working days. / We'd be glad to talk it through on a short call.]

[Future-roles consent wording, if used.]

[name], [role]
[reply address]

## 3. After final interview (the email that confirms the call)

**Subject:** Following our call about the [role] role

Hi [first name],

Thank you for speaking with [me / the hiring manager's role] earlier. As we discussed, we've decided not to offer you the [role] role, and I'm sorry it isn't better news after the time you gave us at [the final interview / the assessment session].

[Written feedback: As promised, I'll send your written feedback by [date]. / If you'd like written feedback, reply by [date].]

[Future-roles consent wording, if used.]

[name], [role]
[reply address]

### Call notes for the person making the call

- Say who you are and why you're calling, then give the decision in the first minute.
- Thank them for one specific thing from the interview.
- Offer written feedback and say when it will arrive. Keep reasons for the written feedback, so the call and the note match.
- Make no comparison with other candidates and no promise about future roles.
- Tell them an email will follow today with the same message.

## 4. Vacancy filled, paused or withdrawn

Hi [first name], [two or three sentences: what has happened to the vacancy, what it means for their application, whether it will reopen if that's known, and who to contact.]

## 5. Holding update

Hi [first name], I said we'd be in touch about the [role] role by [date]. The decision is taking longer than we planned, and you'll hear from me by [new date]. Thank you for your patience.

## 6. Future-roles consent wording (only if you keep details)

We'd like to keep your [application and interview notes] for [period] so we can contact you about [kind of role] at [organisation]. If you're happy for us to do that, reply "yes" to this email. If we don't hear from you, we'll delete your details at the end of our usual [retention period]. You can change your mind at any time by emailing [address]. Our privacy notice is at [link].
