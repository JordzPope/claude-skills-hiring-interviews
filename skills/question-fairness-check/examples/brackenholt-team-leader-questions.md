# Team Leader, Customer Operations: panel questions (draft)

From: contact centre manager, Sheffield
For: the final panel for the four Team Leader roles. Internal and external candidates are interviewed together, by the contact centre manager and an HR adviser, in person or by video. 45 minutes each.

## Criteria (from the Team Leader success profile)

1. Coaches and develops people
2. Delivers results through the team
3. Handles difficult conversations
4. Makes good decisions under pressure

## Questions, in order

1. "Tell me about a time you helped a colleague get better at something." (Coaches and develops people)
2. "Tell me about a time you had to get a group to a goal when everyone was under pressure." (Delivers results through the team)
3. "Have you had much time off sick in the last two years?" (Delivers results through the team)
4. "You'd be comfortable having a tough conversation with someone who used to be your peer, wouldn't you?" (Handles difficult conversations)
5. "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a decision made above you." (Handles difficult conversations)
6. "It's 2pm, the Pulse board is red and you've got two people on a Huddle 3. Talk us through how you'd get the afternoon back to green." (Makes good decisions under pressure)
7. "Team leaders cover the late shift until 9pm one week in four. Have you got childcare sorted for that?" (Delivers results through the team)
8. "What would you like to ask us?" (not scored)

Probes for questions 1, 2 and 5: "What did you do?" "What happened in the end?" "What would you do differently next time?"

Each panel member scores questions 1 to 7 from 1 to 4 on their own, using the Team Leader scoring guide, before the panel talks.
