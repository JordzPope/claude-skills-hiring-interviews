# Smart-Meter Engineer: reference request email and form (current version)

**How we use it:** the recruitment coordinator emails this to the candidate's last two employers, taken from the employment history on their application, on the day the candidate is invited to the practical assessment. Getting references in early means we can make offers in the same week as the assessment.

## Email

Subject: Reference request for [candidate name], Smart-Meter Engineer, Brackenholt Energy

Dear Sir or Madam,

[Candidate name] has applied for a Smart-Meter Engineer role with Brackenholt Energy and is due to attend our practical assessment. Please complete the attached form and return it within five working days to recruitment@[domain].

If your organisation's policy is to confirm dates and job title only, please complete Section 1 and tell us.

Your reply will be read by the recruitment coordinator and the field supervisor, and kept with the recruitment file in line with our privacy notice. The candidate may be able to see what you write if they ask for their data under data protection law.

Kind regards,
Recruitment coordinator, Brackenholt Energy

8. How many days of sickness absence did they have in their last 12 months with you?
9. Are you aware of any back, knee or other health problems that could affect their ability to kneel, lift or work at height?
