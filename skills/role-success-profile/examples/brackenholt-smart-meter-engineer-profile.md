## What the job involves

Engineers work from home and a van across Yorkshire, the North East and the North West, with six to eight booked appointments a day in customers' homes and small business premises. Applicants need a dual-fuel smart-metering qualification and a full driving licence. New starters have a two-week induction on Brackenholt's meters, van stock and job app.

- Exchange old gas and electricity meters for smart meters, and commission them so readings reach Brackenholt
- Make each installation safe: isolate the supply, carry out a gas tightness test after any gas work, and stop work and report any dangerous gas or electrical situation found, under the Brackenholt field safety procedure
- Explain the new meter and the in-home display to the customer before leaving, including how to read it and who to call
- Record each job, with photos and readings, on the job app before the next appointment
- Plan the day's route with the planners, and tell them as soon as a job will overrun

## Criteria

1. **Passionate about the energy transition.** A resilient self-starter who thrives on the road.
2. **Fits and commissions smart meters to the field manual standard.** Completes exchanges correctly first time; confirms commissioning before leaving.
3. **Explains the new meter clearly to customers in their homes.** Checks the customer can read the display; adapts for customers who find technology hard.
4. **Excellent communication skills with customers.** Builds rapport on the doorstep and keeps customers informed during the visit.
5. **Plans their own day.** Works the route out with the planners and tells them early when a job will overrun.
6. **Long-term commitment.** Will stay with Brackenholt for at least five years.
