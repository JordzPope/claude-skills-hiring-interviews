# Scoring guide: Team Leader, Customer Operations (final interview)

Drafted by: contact centre manager, with the talent acquisition partner, for the four Team Leader roles in the Sheffield contact centre. Internal and external candidates sit the same interview.

Panel: two contact centre managers and the talent acquisition partner.
Scale: 1 to 5. Anchors at 1, 3 and 5; use 2 or 4 when an answer sits between them.

Criteria from the role success profile:

1. Coaching advisers to improve
2. Planning the team's day
3. Handling a difficult conversation
4. Dealing with complaints escalated to the team leader
5. Culture fit

## How to score

Score each answer on your own, straight after the interview, using your notes and the anchors below. Don't discuss candidates with the rest of the panel until everyone's scores are in. Write one line of evidence next to each score.

What doesn't count as evidence: appearance, accent, nerves, and anything about a protected characteristic. An agreed adjustment, such as having the questions in advance, never lowers a score.

## Coaching advisers to improve

Question: Tell us about a time you helped someone get better at part of their job.
Probes: What did you notice first? What did you say to them? What changed, and how did you know?

| Score | The answer contains |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | A specific example where they spotted a gap from evidence (call listening, quality scores, a customer comment), agreed one change with the person, followed up more than once, and can say what improved and how they knew. Talks about what they did themselves. |
| 3 | A real example with clear steps they took, but the follow-up is thin or the result is vague ("they got better"). |
| 1 | No example, or a general view of what coaching should be; or the "help" was telling the person off or doing the work for them. |

## Planning the team's day

Question: Your team of 12 is three people down at 8:45 and the queue is already building. Talk us through the first hour.
Probes: Who do you speak to first? What do you stop or move? How do you tell the team?

| Score | The answer contains |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Excellent planning. |
| 3 | Good planning, with some gaps. |
| 1 | Poor planning. |

## Handling a difficult conversation

Question: Tell us about a time you had to tell someone something they didn't want to hear.
Probes: How did you prepare? What did you actually say? What happened afterwards?

| Score | The answer contains |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Confident, articulate and polished throughout. Comes across as a natural leader with real presence, and answers without hesitating. |
| 3 | A real example where they prepared, said the hard thing plainly and listened to the response, though they say little about what happened next. |
| 1 | Avoided the conversation or passed it to someone else, or describes saying it in a way that made things worse. |

## Culture fit

Question: Why Brackenholt?
Probes: What do you know about how we work here?

| Score | The answer contains |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Feels like one of us already. Knows how things work here and would slot straight into the team leader group. |
| 3 | Would get on with the team. |
| 1 | Not really a Brackenholt person. |
