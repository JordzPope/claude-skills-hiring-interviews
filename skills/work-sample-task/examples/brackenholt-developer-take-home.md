# Software Developer: take-home task (draft)

From: engineering manager, Technology
For: candidates who pass the first video interview for the two remote Software Developer roles
Sent by: the recruitment coordinator, through the ATS

## Criteria this task tests (from the Software Developer success profile)

1. **Writes clear, working code:** code a teammate can read, with tests for the main cases.
2. **Thinks about the customer:** handles the cases that would hurt a customer if they went wrong.
3. **Explains technical choices plainly:** can say why they built it this way to someone outside the team.

## Brief for candidates

**The task.** Our billing tool tries to collect each direct debit once. If the bank rejects it, someone in the team chases it by hand. Build a small service that retries failed direct-debit payments on a sensible schedule, knows when to stop, and lets the customer know what's happening. This is the next item on our billing backlog, so it's a realistic test of the work you'd do here.

**What you'll get.** A starter repository with a fake payments API and a set of made-up failed-payment records. Everything in it is invented for this task.

**Time.** There's no time limit. Most strong candidates spend a weekend on it, and we love it when people go beyond the brief.

**AI tools.** You can use any AI tools you'd use at work. Tell us in your README which ones you used and what for.

**Adjustments.** If you'd like extra time, the brief in a different format or anything else that would help, email the recruitment coordinator before you start. You don't need to tell us why.

**Sending it in.** Push your work to a private repository and share it with the address in your invitation within two weeks of getting this brief.

**How it's marked.** We'll look at how clear and well tested your code is, how you've handled the cases that matter to customers, and how well your README explains your choices.

**What happens next.** We review every submission offline, and the talent acquisition partner emails you the outcome within five working days. We use your work only to assess you, and we delete it six months after the roles are filled.

## Marking guide (for markers only)

| What we look at | Mark |
| --- | --- |
| Overall impression of the code | 1 to 10 |
| Would I enjoy working with this person? | 1 to 10 |
| Bonus for anything that impresses | up to 5 |

Two developers from the team mark each submission on their own, record their marks in the ATS, then compare.
